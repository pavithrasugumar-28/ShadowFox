Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear answers to your most common dental care concerns.

1. Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?

No. Medications may offer short-term relief, but long-term relief requires treating the root cause with professional dental care.

2. Does teeth scaling cause enamel damage, sensitivity, or gaps?

No. Scaling removes plaque safely without harming enamel. Some temporary sensitivity may occur but usually fades as gums heal.

3. Can fluorosis stains be removed through scaling?

Scaling removes surface stains, but fluorosis—being internal—requires cosmetic treatments like bleaching or veneers.

4. Can painful teeth be treated with fillings?

Only if the decay hasn’t reached the pulp. Advanced cases may need a root canal or extraction instead.

5. Is a crown necessary after a root canal?

Yes. A crown protects the tooth and prevents fractures after RCT, especially during chewing.

6. Can milk teeth need root canal treatment?

Yes, if they’re deeply decayed and not ready to fall out. It preserves the tooth and prevents infection.

7. How can I maintain good oral hygiene?

Brush twice a day, floss, rinse after meals, and visit your dentist regularly for check-ups and cleaning.

8. What is the best age for braces?

Usually between 12 and 14 years, but early intervention may be helpful in some cases.

9. Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No. Only impacted, infected, or painful ones due to crowding or complications need removal.

10. Is it important to replace missing teeth?

Yes. It helps maintain bite alignment and prevents adjacent teeth from shifting.

11. What are the benefits of dental implants?

They restore appearance and function, improving confidence and chewing ability.

12. Which type of toothbrush and toothpaste should I use?

Use a soft or medium brush with fluoride toothpaste. Replace it every 3 months.

13. What causes tooth sensitivity?

It may be due to enamel wear, exposed roots, decay, or micro-cracks in the teeth.

14. How often should I visit a dentist?

Every six months for regular cleanings and preventive care.
