Meet the Heart Behind the Smiles

Discover the story, vision, and dedicated team powering compassionate dental care at Sakthi Dental Clinic.

Dr. Anupriya – Founder & Chief Dentist

With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Anupriya established Sakthi Dental Clinic to make expert dental care accessible to all. A graduate of Government Dental College, she blends clinical excellence with heartfelt care—evident in every treatment and every patient’s smile.

Her journey has spanned private practice, public service, and community outreach—shaping the clinic's mission of trust, quality, and accessibility.
